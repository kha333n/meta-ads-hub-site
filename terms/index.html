Meta Ads Hub is a private web app run by Usman Khan. A small team uses it to set up and manage Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns, for its own ad accounts and those of its clients, through Meta's Marketing API. These terms apply to everyone who uses it and to every business whose ad account it manages.

Who may use it

Only people the operator has given an account. It isn't offered to the public, and accounts can't be shared.

Ad accounts

  • The app works only on ad accounts their owner has given it access to in Meta Business settings. The owner can take that access away at any time.
  • Everything the app makes in Meta starts paused. An ad runs only after a person on the team turns it on.
  • The ad account's owner pays Meta for its ads, under Meta's own terms.

Using it properly

  • Ads made with the app must follow Meta's Advertising Standards and the law where they run.
  • No one may use it to reach an ad account they have no right to, or to get around Meta's limits.

Meta

The app uses Meta's Marketing API. Meta Ads Hub is not made or endorsed by Meta, and Meta's own terms govern the ad accounts, Pages and ads themselves. When Meta changes or refuses something, the app shows what Meta said.
