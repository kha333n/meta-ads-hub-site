Meta Ads Hub

Deleting your data

Last updated 31 October 2026

Meta Ads Hub doesn't use Facebook Login, so it holds nothing about people who use Facebook or Instagram. It holds data only about the ad accounts a business has given it access to, as the privacy policy describes.

Remove the app's access

In Meta Business settings, take the ad account, Page and Instagram account away from the system user the app uses, or remove Meta Ads Hub from your business's connected apps. From then on the app can't read or change anything in that ad account.

Have your data deleted

  1. Email kha333n@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", from an address of the business that owns the ad account.
  2. Give the ad account's id: the number in Ads Manager's address, after act=.

Within 30 days, everything the app holds for that ad account is deleted: its copies of the campaigns, ad sets, ads and their numbers, the pictures and videos uploaded for its ads, the change log, the shop totals, and the access token. You get an email when it's done.

Nothing in your Meta account changes: your campaigns and ads stay as they are in Ads Manager.
